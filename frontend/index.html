A few things to know.

Use the keypad or type with your keyboard. Press Enter to calculate and save to your notebook.

Arithmetic
+ − * / % and ^ for powers. Use * explicitly: 2*pi.
Scientific functions
sin, cos, tan, asin, acos, atan, sqrt, ln, log (base 10), exp, abs, floor, ceil, factorial.
Constants & angles
pi, e, tau. RAD uses radians; DEG uses degrees. Changing modes clears the result until you calculate again.
Your notebook
Reuse an expression, give it a label, or delete it. Saved in PostgreSQL for this shared classroom demo.
Precision
Real numbers with floating-point precision. Expressions are limited to 256 characters. Factorial supports integers 0–170.
